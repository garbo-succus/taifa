Designers: Víctor Catalá, Alex Folch, Ignacio Torres and Jesús Peralta. Graphic design: Jesús Peralta.

Official English print-and-play source: https://printandplay.games/taifa-eng/

The source map, counter sheets and rulebook page 15 bear **CC BY-NC-SA**. The printed notice does not identify a version; none has been invented here. Preserve this notice and the creator credits. This package covers the original print-and-play artwork, not the later Worthington commercial edition.

Changes: separated cards and two-sided counters, removed off-viewport objects and unused SVG definitions, outlined PDF text, retained physical dimensions and losslessly re-encoded embedded raster images. Original PDF files remain unchanged in `sources/pnp/`. Low-resolution artwork parts were enhanced with Real-CUGAN; runtime artwork uses AVIF/WebP compression.
